# 👩🏻‍🔬 Francine Almeida

**`Bioinformatician | Population Genomics | Genome Assembly`**

I’m a biologist (UFES, 2016), with an M.Sc. in Biotechnology (UFES, 2018) and a Ph.D. in Genetics and Plant Breeding (UFES, 2023). During my Ph.D., I completed a research exchange at the University of Gothenburg (Sweden), where I continued my work and established new collaborations. I currently work as a Bioinformatician at the Genomics for Climate Change Research Center (GCCRC), focusing on plant genome assembly and curation, population genomics, and reproducible research best practices.
